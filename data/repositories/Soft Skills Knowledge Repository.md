Soft Skills Knowledge Repository
Purpose

This document defines the core professional competencies, interpersonal skills, leadership qualities, and workplace behaviors expected of candidates across technical and non-technical roles. It is intended to serve as a knowledge base for an AI Interview Assistant to generate personalized behavioral and situational interview questions based on:

Job Description (JD)
Candidate Resume
Years of Experience
Target Role
Leadership Expectations

The assistant should use this repository to generate competency-based, behavioral, situational, leadership, communication, and collaboration questions while adapting the depth according to the candidate's experience and role.

Guidance for the AI Interview Assistant

When using this repository:

Tailor behavioral questions to the role and seniority. For an individual contributor, focus on collaboration, communication, accountability, and problem-solving. For leads and managers, emphasize leadership, mentoring, stakeholder management, and strategic decision-making.
Use resume-driven personalization. If the resume mentions leading a team, mentoring engineers, coordinating releases, or interacting with clients, ask follow-up questions that validate those experiences.
Favor competency-based and situational questions. Ask candidates to describe real experiences using approaches such as the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result), rather than asking for definitions of leadership or Agile concepts.
Assess outcomes, not just activities. Encourage candidates to explain the impact of their actions, lessons learned, and how they measured success or handled failures.
Balance technical and interpersonal evaluation. Even for highly technical roles, include questions that explore communication, collaboration, ownership, and adaptability, as these are essential for success in modern engineering teams.
